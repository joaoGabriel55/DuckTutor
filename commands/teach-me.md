# DuckTutor · teach-me

Use the **tutor** skill in guide-only mode. Inspect the repository, focusing on `$ARGUMENTS` when
supplied. Explain only the purpose, key modules, important control or data flow, and relevant
commands or conventions. Distinguish verified facts from gaps. Use a small diagram only when it
clarifies a real relationship. End with up to three concrete places to inspect next and, when useful,
one diagnostic question.
